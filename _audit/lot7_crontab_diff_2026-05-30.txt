Notes on the diff:
  - All `PROPR_API_KEY=...`, `--private-key <HEX>`, `TELEGRAM_*=...` inline
    secrets are removed from `crontab -l`. They live in `.env` and are sourced
    by `run_with_env.sh` before each command.
  - `"$VAR"` quoting protects against accidental word-splitting if a value
    contains whitespace.
  - Trailing redirects standardize stderr+stdout to a per-job log file.
  - Adding `>> ~/.../arxiv_fetch.log` for fetch_arxiv.sh fixes the silent-fail
    bug (V2 line 42: log was absent for that job).
  - The `@reboot` line is unchanged: it spawns MT5 in a detached screen which
    reads its own .ini — no secret in the cron itself.
