## Verdict & flags (honest)
1. **Launch timing is the free lever:** avoid Q1 (Jan-Feb ~4-8% single-try); prefer **April** or **October**.
   Don't wait for a vol regime — it doesn't move p(pass).
2. **J1/USDJPY has decayed** (2022 peak → ~flat 2024-26). The mods (suspension gate/LCAP8) are correct tail
   management but the edge is thin; J1 now pays via decorrelation, not return. Worth deciding if it still
   earns its 25% variance / margin footprint, or should be down-weighted vs gold.
3. **Gold carries the book** (65% variance, +2.99 skew) — it is the engine; protect its exposure.
4. **Caveats:** ONE market history (wide CIs); month-level N≈24-32 (noisy — trust quarters, not months);
