# RESULTS H1/H2 — Trend & breakout as the growth engine

**Verdict: the best retail futures/forex directional edge (diversified trend) delivers ~7-10%/yr growth at
full Kelly with ~0% ruin — a SAFE, SLOW engine + crisis-hedge, NOT a fast-growth engine. The 30-80%/yr
target (let alone doubling/6mo) is NOT reachable, bounded by the growth law g = SR²/2 and the weak modern
trend Sharpe (~0.4).** Universe: 18-19 futures (equity/rates/energy/metals/grains/FX), 2007-2026.

## The two decisive findings

**1. The growth law is the wall: g_max = SR²/2.** No amount of leverage beats it (over-Kelly REDUCES
growth and raises ruin). At SR 0.44, full-Kelly growth = **10%/yr**. To reach 30%/yr you need SR 0.77;
80%/yr → SR 1.26; double/6mo (139%/yr) → **SR 1.67**. Retail diversified trend maxes at SR ~0.4-0.5.

**2. Modern trend is weak (SR 0.35-0.44), consistent with the documented post-2011 CTA drought**
(AQR: TSMOM Sharpe fell from ~0.8 pre-2011 to ~0.3-0.5). This is NOT a construction failure — class-
balancing, continuous vs sign signals, faster/slower lookbacks, and the ensemble all land 0.16-0.44.

**Ruin is ~0% at all Kelly fractions** — the engine is *very safe* but *very slow*. The growth-max sizing
within the P(ruin)<25% budget is ~full Kelly → **~7-10%/yr**. Over-betting past full Kelly cannot raise
growth (Kelly theorem).

## What H1/H2 ARE good for
- **Positive monthly skew (+0.6) + crisis-alpha** (2008 +7%, 2020 +6%, 2022 +5%) → a genuine **ruin-hedge
  / crisis-protection overlay**. Two-sided short-leg is flat on average but profits exactly in crashes.
- Combined with the free bond base (~2%/yr): a ~10-15%/yr low-ruin defensive book.

## €12k feasibility caveat
The engine needs ~19 micro futures. At €12k, full-Kelly (12×) = ~€144k notional / 19 = ~€7.5k each —
**below 1 micro contract for most (MNQ notional ~$40k)**. You can hold only a SUBSET (~5-8 smallest micros:
M2K, MCL, MYM, micro-yield, M6E…) → less diversification → SR drops further. The full engine needs ~€30-50k.

## Honest verdict for the goal
**No validated retail futures/forex directional edge grows a €12k account at 30-80%/yr.** The growth law
makes it unavoidable: fast growth needs SR ≥ 0.8, and modern retail directional trend/breakout tops out at
SR ~0.4 → ~10%/yr. This is the SAME fundamental scarcity as the Sharpe quest, now proven via g = SR²/2.
The honest choice: **(a) accept a real ~10-15%/yr low-ruin engine** (trend + bond base, doubles in ~5-6 yr),
or **(b) chase fast growth via concentrated high-variance bets = gambling, not edge** (high ruin, violates
the mandate). H3 (risk-off overlay = H1's already-crisis-positive short leg) and H4 (FX trend, dead
post-Menkhoff per repo) will not lift the SR-0.4 ceiling.
