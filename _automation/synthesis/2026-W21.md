**Origin**: Combines three practitioner sources from Theme T4: EGARCH paper (static rolling vol lags crash regimes) + Mozes (vol-spike → crash mechanism, directly caused v3 MaxDD breach) + regime-dependent allocation paper (regime-conditional sizing improves trend Sharpe).
