## 4. Orthogonal hypotheses

### [H007] Crypto perpetual tick-size/vol ratio as a leading indicator of Strategy A's structural trend-survival ceiling
**Statement**: Compute the tick-size-normalized-by-volatility ratio for BTC/ETH/SOL perpetuals
(HL) and compare it against the small-tick/large-tick threshold implied by the CFM mechanism
(2607.01550) that separates "trend dead" from "trend alive" instruments in traditional futures.

**Origin**: 2607.01550 (CFM, W27) establishes tick-size/vol as the variable separating trend
survival from collapse in traditional futures post-2009, explicitly rejecting capacity/crowding
explanations. No paper this window (or ever, per their own abstract) tests whether the mechanism
transfers to crypto perpetuals.

**Economic mechanism**: HL/Binance perpetuals have effectively continuous tick sizes relative to
price (tick size is a tiny fraction of typical BTC/ETH/SOL volatility) — by the CFM mechanism this
should place crypto squarely in the "small tick → impact channel broken → trend dead" bucket, yet
Strategy A shows Sharpe 1.33 OOS. Two non-exclusive readings: (a) crypto trend survives via a
different channel entirely (market immaturity, retail/momentum-chasing order flow not yet
crowded out by CTA-style capital) — in which case Strategy A's edge has a different, possibly more
fragile, half-life than assumed; or (b) the tick-size mechanism doesn't generalize outside the
traditional-futures microstructure the authors studied — informative null. Either answer sharpens
the risk read on a strategy that already breached once (2026-05-11 Propr breach) and is currently frozen.

**Data required**: HL perpetual tick sizes (fetchable via HL API, not yet in `data/`) + existing
vol_28d pipeline (BTC/ETH/SOL, already computed for Strategy A). No new market data fetch beyond
tick-size metadata.

**Cost to test**: LOW — a diagnostic ratio calculation, not a backtest. Under an hour.

**Conflict with archived?** None. Strategy A is live/frozen, not archived; this is a risk
diagnostic, not a new signal, and doesn't touch the 3-modification budget.

**Strategy fit**: A (methodology / risk diagnostic).

No second or third hypothesis this week — the other candidate patterns (regime-conditioning
methods, FRI decomposition applied to NQ) either overlap materially with already-open hypotheses
(H002/H004/H005) or carry a self-flagged high probability of a null-by-construction result
(§3, Strategy C backlog note). Padding to 3 for its own sake would violate the "no filler" rule.
