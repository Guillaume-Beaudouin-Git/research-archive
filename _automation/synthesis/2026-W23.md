**Origin:**
- 2605.30442: fiat turbulence is the primary stress propagation channel; crypto assets lag fiat in network reconfiguration during stress events.
- Mechanism extension: if fiat stress (VIX) leads crypto repricing, it also leads the leveraged-long demand collapse that compresses funding rates. This creates a usable 12-24h window.

VIX expansion (>25) identifies the onset of this sequence with historical regularity across the COVID-2020, crypto-winter-2022, and SVB-2023 episodes.
