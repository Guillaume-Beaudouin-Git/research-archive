**Link** : https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19497

variants) into a single analytical framework, relating expected PnL to the autocorrelation structure

**Authors** : Robert Jacob Ryan
**Link** : https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01494
**Submitted** : 2026-08-02

the project's explicit focus areas.
