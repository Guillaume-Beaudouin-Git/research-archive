## Summary
Agentic AI deployments create a coverage gap in commercial insurance: some AI-mediated losses are affirmatively insured, others create silent exposure under legacy cyber, E&O, D&O, EPL, and GL policies, and some remain uninsurable. The paper maps this frontier using a taxonomy from 600 publicly disclosed AI incidents (2016-2024) evaluated against standard actuarial insurability criteria including uncertainty, randomness, maximum possible loss, and moral hazard.
