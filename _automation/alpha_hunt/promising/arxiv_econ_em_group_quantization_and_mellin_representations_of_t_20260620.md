## Summary
The Heston stochastic volatility model is recast via lifted local Lie groupoids. The Riccati equations in momentum space and the Heston pricing operator in coordinate space are shown to be two representations of the same groupoid construction. The paper provides geometric insight into the Heston affine-transform structure without proposing new pricing formulas.
