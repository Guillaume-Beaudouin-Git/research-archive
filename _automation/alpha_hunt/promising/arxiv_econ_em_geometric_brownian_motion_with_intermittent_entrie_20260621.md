## Summary
A generalization of geometric Brownian motion that incorporates asymmetric entry and exit rates for population units (firms, workers, assets), extending stochastic resetting models. Despite asymmetry, the system reaches a stationary distribution characterized by three distinct regimes depending on the interplay of volatility, drift, entry, and exit rates. The paper derives survival probability and mean first-passage time, and identifies an optimal exit rate minimizing the mean first-passage time — suggesting that calibrated exit policies can materially affect system outcomes.
