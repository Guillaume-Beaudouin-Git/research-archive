## Summary
A review of Cohen, Malloy, and Nguyen's "Lazy Prices" paper, which finds that small year-on-year changes in 10-K filings predict large negative returns. The mechanism: companies that change their 10-K language significantly are signaling underlying business change, while those that copy-paste the prior year are on autopilot. The paper claims 22% alpha from a strategy shorting companies with high 10-K text similarity to prior year.
