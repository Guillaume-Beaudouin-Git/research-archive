## Summary
Standard impact assessments of maritime chokepoint disruptions overestimate economic costs because they ignore adaptive rerouting by profit-maximizing firms. The authors build an equilibrium model of global shipping that incorporates route-choice responses under disruption risk across 14 key chokepoints. The correction is largest for severe disruptions where adaptive behavior is most consequential.
