## Summary
On real market data, chart visual evidence and price trend are strongly correlated, making it impossible to distinguish a model that genuinely reads candlestick patterns from one that simply extrapolates trend momentum. The authors prove this identification limitation formally, then construct Martingale Doppelgänger pairs: synthetic charts with identical trend statistics but independently re-sampled local candlestick features under the risk-neutral measure. A model genuinely reading charts should perform equally on real and Doppelgänger charts; a trend extrapolator performs worse on Doppelgängers. Empirical evaluation of several frontier VLMs finds substantial performance degradation on Doppelgängers, concluding that most current VLMs extrapolate trends rather than read chart patterns.
