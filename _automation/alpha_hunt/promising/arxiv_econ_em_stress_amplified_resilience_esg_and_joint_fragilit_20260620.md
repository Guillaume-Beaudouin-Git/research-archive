## Summary
Using monthly S&P 500 data from 2014 to 2025, ESG ratings are shown to predict lower cofragility exposure — the joint occurrence of drawdown, volatility spike, and illiquidity in the same firm-month — rather than unconditional return premiums. The ESG return association is conditional on stress states and absent or reversed in calm markets.
