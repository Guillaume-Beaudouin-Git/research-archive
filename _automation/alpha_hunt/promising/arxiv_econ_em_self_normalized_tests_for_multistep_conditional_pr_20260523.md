## Summary
This paper proposes self-normalized tests for multistep conditional predictive ability in forecast comparison. By normalizing the sample mean of the transformed loss differential using functionals of its cumulative sum (CUSUM) process, specifically an adjusted-range normalizer for scalars and a matrix normalizer for vectors, our approach avoids direct estimation of the long-run covariance matrix.
