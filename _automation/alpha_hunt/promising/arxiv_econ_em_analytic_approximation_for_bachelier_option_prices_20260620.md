## Summary
Under the Bachelier model with zero correlation between prices and volatilities, the implied volatility equals the fair value of the volatility swap. OTM and ITM option prices are expanded as series in moneyness, with coefficients involving negative non-integer powers of future mean volatility. The expansion is used as a control variate in Monte Carlo pricing for the correlated case, substantially reducing simulation variance.
