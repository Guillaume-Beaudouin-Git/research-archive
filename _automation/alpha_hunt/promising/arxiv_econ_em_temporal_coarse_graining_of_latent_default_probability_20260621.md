## Summary
Persistent dynamics of a latent default-probability path generate apparent default correlation through temporal coarse-graining, even when defaults are conditionally independent at monthly frequency. In the OU-Binomial baseline, observed quarterly or annual default correlation is proportional to the autocorrelation of the latent path. This has implications for CDO tranche pricing and bank stress testing: measured correlation may be a measurement-horizon artifact.
