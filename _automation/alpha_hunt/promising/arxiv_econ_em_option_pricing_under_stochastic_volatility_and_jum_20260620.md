## Summary
A PIDE framework combines stochastic volatility and jump dynamics using the Lévy-type affine process generator, implemented via FD with FFT-based jump treatment. GMM calibration on S&P 500 options at three maturities shows stochastic volatility dominates jump contributions — Heston alone captures the majority of Black-Scholes pricing error at medium maturities.
