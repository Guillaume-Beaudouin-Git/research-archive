## Summary
A theoretical model showing that discrete block-time clearing and paid-priority fee ordering undermine the viability of blockchain-based price formation. Paid priority creates endogenous participation cutoffs where only high-valuation traders participate, reducing price discovery quality. Longer block times (better security) amplify these effects and can cause markets to shut down. The theoretical framework predicts that block-time-based exchanges suffer from structural adverse selection concentrated in a single clearing round.
