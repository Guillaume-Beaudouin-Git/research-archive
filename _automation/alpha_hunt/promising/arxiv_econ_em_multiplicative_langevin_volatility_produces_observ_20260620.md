## Summary
The empirical Q-variance relationship — that E(σ²|z) is linear in z² — is shown to be exactly equivalent to an Inverse Gamma distribution for volatility, which in turn is generated by a multiplicative Langevin process with a settable coherence time. This provides a first-principles derivation of the variance-return dependence from a simple stochastic process without ad-hoc distributional assumptions.

The paper states the Q-variance identity: $\mathbf{E}(\sigma^2 | z) = \sigma_0^2 + \frac{1}{2}z^2$, where $\sigma^2$ is observed volatility in interval $T$ and $z$ is the suitably scaled return in the same interval.
