## Summary
Optimal portfolio allocation is characterized for a household simultaneously managing a random-deadline precautionary goal and a fixed-deadline accumulation goal. A growth crowding-out effect and a dead-capital effect emerge from the two-goal structure. Closed-form solutions are derived in a Black-Scholes market under a forced funding rule.
