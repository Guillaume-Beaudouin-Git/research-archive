## Summary
An integrated framework for stress-testing loan portfolios combines loan production simulation with a dynamic Basel IRB credit risk model using time-varying PD and LGD. Correlated defaults are handled through a multi-factor Gaussian copula. The framework is validated on US mortgage and corporate loan data from 2005-2020, covering the GFC stress period.
