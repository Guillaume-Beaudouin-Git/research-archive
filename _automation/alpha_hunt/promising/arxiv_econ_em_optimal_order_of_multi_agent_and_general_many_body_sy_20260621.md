## Summary
A general framework for multi-agent systems with feedback loops analyzes macroscopic properties (total power, useful power, entropy, order, fragility, mobility) as emergent from agent-level power and response functions. Applications include price impact models, liquidity dynamics, and systemic risk in interbank networks. The framework unifies agent-influence (power) and reaction-to-observations (response) into a coherent theory of collective outcomes.
