## Summary
A model for institutional portfolio crossing platforms (dark pools where institutional investors cross large portfolio trades) where investors report preferences as portfolios rather than individual securities. The platform uses hybrid demand-query + value-query design: demand queries search the portfolio space, value queries verify specific packages. The hybrid procedure recovers 88% of full-information welfare vs ~50% for demand-only or value-only designs. Empirical experiments use equity panels from the US, Korea, Japan, and Germany.
