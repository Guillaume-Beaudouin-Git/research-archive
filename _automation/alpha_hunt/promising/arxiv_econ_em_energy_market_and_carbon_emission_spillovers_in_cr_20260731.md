## Summary
Uses a time-varying-parameter VAR to measure dynamic volatility spillovers between seven critical-mineral ETFs and macro variables (energy, carbon, sentiment, infrastructure) from 2013-2023, split at the COVID crash. Finds WTI crude and carbon futures are consistent "net receivers" of shocks while cobalt and aluminum ETFs are "net givers" (transmitters), with the pandemic causing a structural shift in these roles, and argues high-ESG-score portfolios contribute disproportionately to shock transmission.
