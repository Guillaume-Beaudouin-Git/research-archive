## Summary
A three-currency HJM no-arbitrage framework where nominal, real (inflation-indexed), and corporate credit are linked through synthetic exchange rates. The testable identity: corporate spread over inflation-indexed risk-free = corporate spread over nominal risk-free + model-implied breakeven inflation forward. Applied to Brazilian CDI-indexed and IPCA-indexed debentures (15 large issuers, Jan 2021 – Feb 2026), finding a systematic triangle residual averaging 640 basis points at 3-year tenor — stable across BCB tightening (2021-2023) and easing (2024-2026) cycles, suggesting a structural segmentation between the two bond segments rather than arbitrage.
