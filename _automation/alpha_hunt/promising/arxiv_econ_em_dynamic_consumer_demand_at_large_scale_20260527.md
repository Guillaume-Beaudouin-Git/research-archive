## Summary
This paper addresses scalable demand estimation in high-dimensional retail environments (many products, multiple categories, repeated purchases) by proposing a dynamic product-level factor model that captures consumer heterogeneity in baseline preferences, price sensitivity, and purchase inertia through a shared latent factor structure. The model is estimated via Bayesian variational inference, enabling tractable computation with tens of thousands of parameters. Simulation results show substantial predictive gains over static benchmarks, especially when individual purchase histories are sparse.
