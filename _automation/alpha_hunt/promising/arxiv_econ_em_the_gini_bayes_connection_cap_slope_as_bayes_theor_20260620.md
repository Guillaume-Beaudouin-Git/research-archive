## Summary
The local slope of the cumulative accuracy profile (CAP) is shown to equal the posterior default probability at that score percentile, formally connecting CAP analysis to Bayes' theorem. Multiple industry-standard implementations are reconciled and the area under the power curve is proved to equal the probability that a random defaulter scores above a random non-defaulter.
