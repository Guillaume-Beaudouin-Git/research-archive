## Summary
Capped-usage SaaS products (LLM subscriptions, cloud platforms, corporate benefit platforms) share structural features with insurance: fixed premium decoupled from usage, liability cap, and stochastic demand. The paper maps these products to capped insurance contracts and uses actuarial tools — risk measures, convex order, stop-loss bounds — to derive pricing bounds and characterize the role of usage heterogeneity in determining optimal cap and price.
