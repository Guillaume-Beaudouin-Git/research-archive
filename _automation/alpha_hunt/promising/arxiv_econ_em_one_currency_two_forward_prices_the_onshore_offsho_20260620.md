## Summary
China's CNY (onshore) and CNH (offshore deliverable) forward markets display persistent, predictable deviations despite tightly linked spot prices. A stochastic term-structure model calibrated to the cross-market basis shows mean-reverting deviations subject to regime shifts around policy events. Capital flow frictions and differential access to domestic monetary policy drive the basis.
