## Summary
Under Mechkov's fast-reversion limit of the classical Heston model, price processes exhibit instantaneous excursions through different volatility regimes, consistent with empirical rapid volatility switching. Closed-form option pricing formulas are derived for this fast-excursion Heston model and calibrated to S&P 500 implied volatility surfaces, with better short-term smile fit than standard Heston.
