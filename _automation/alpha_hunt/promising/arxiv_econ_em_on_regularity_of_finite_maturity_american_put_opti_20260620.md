## Summary
The C^{1,2} regularity of American put option value functions under the Heston model is established despite the operator being degenerate when volatility reaches zero. The smooth-fit principle is proved using PDE techniques. This provides rigorous mathematical foundations for numerical Heston American option pricing algorithms.
