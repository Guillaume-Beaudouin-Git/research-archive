## Summary
Portfolio optimization under volatility uncertainty is studied using sublinear expectation, which models ambiguity about the vol process. The framework tracks both maximum and minimum risk via a coupled model parameterized by a single risk factor. The Pareto frontier under long-only constraints is characterized analytically, showing how vol uncertainty shifts the efficient frontier relative to the classical Markowitz case.
