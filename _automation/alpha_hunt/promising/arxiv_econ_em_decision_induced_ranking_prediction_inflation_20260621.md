## Summary
Smart Predict-then-Optimize (SPO) trains predictors to maximize downstream portfolio decision quality rather than prediction accuracy, but this paper shows it produces inflated return signals and unstable reallocations. Via a KKT-based analysis, portfolio decisions are shown to correspond to rankings over risk- and transaction-cost-adjusted marginal scores; SPO training amplifies rank differences between near-optimal and sub-optimal assets, increasing prediction variance and driving turnover. A modified SPO variant that penalizes prediction spread reduces turnover by 30% while maintaining portfolio performance.
