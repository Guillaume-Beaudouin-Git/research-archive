## Summary
This study provides a consistent, multi-hazard assessment of failure risk across 13,000+ line segments and 10,000+ substations in the US high-voltage grid, spanning nine natural hazard types plus one compound scenario. Expected daily damage estimates range from $137M/day (tropical cyclone wind) down to $34M/day (landslide). Downstream economic output losses peak at $4.93B/day for tornadoes and $3.59B/day for floods. A 250-year geomagnetic storm produces damage comparable to major terrestrial hazards ($2.07B/day). The compound freezing-rain-plus-wind-gust scenario is the upper-bound stress test at $85.16B/day with 237M people affected.
