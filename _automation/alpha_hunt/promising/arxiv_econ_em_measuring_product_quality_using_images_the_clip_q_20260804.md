## Summary
Introduces "CLIP Q-score," a method for scoring product quality from images using contrastive language-image pretraining embeddings, applied to roughly 500,000 real-estate listing photos. Shows the score predicts sale/rental prices and correlates with faster time-on-market (better liquidity) for listed properties.
