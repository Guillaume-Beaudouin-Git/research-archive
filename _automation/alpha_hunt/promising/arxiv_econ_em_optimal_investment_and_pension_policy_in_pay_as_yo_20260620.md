Optimal investment and pension policy is characterized for a PAYG system with a buffer fund used for intergenerational risk-sharing. Preferences are modeled as non-zero volatility forward CRRA utilities accounting for sustainability and adequacy. Closed-form solutions are derived with numerical sensitivity analysis of preference parameters.
