## Summary
Alpha Architect examines what happens when a large share of systematic capital converges on the same documented factor patterns in equities. The argument is that the persistent statistical regularities linking firm characteristics to future returns — size, value, momentum, quality — are subject to capacity constraints once they become widely traded. As money flows into factor vehicles, the price signals that originally generated the anomaly get arbitraged away, raising the question of which factor implementations retain exploitable edge and which have become structurally crowded.
