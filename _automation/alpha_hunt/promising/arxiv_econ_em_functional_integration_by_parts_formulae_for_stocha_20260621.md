## Summary
The paper derives two types of integration by parts (IBP) formulae for stochastic Volterra equations: an intrinsic one using only the non-anticipative derivative (generalizing classical Brownian motion IBP), and a second relying on kernel regularization of the Volterra process. Both formulae involve a directional derivative with respect to the flat extension of the kernel, constituting an intermediate step toward functional Itô calculus for Volterra processes.
