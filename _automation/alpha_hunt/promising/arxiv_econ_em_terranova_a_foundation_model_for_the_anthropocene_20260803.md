## Summary
Introduces a foundation model jointly trained on 512 gridded Earth-system physical fields and 512 national societal/economic indicators, using dedicated encoders and cross-modal transformers to fuse continuous geophysical data with administrative-unit societal data, with a hypernetwork-generated decoder producing calibrated predictive distributions. Shown to be competitive with purpose-built geospatial models while additionally covering time, oceans, and uncertainty, and to adapt to unseen variables on consumer hardware.
