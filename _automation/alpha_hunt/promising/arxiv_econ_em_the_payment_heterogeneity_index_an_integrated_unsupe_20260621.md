## Summary
PHI (Payment Heterogeneity Index) is an unsupervised anomaly detection score for public procurement fraud combining Benford's law analysis, clustering deviation metrics, round-number concentration tests, and contractor network graph measures. Applied to a Portuguese procurement dataset with confirmed fraud labels, PHI achieves 83% precision and 71% recall on anomalous payment patterns.
