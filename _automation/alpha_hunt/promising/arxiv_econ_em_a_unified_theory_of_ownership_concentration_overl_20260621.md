## Summary
Ownership concentration in investor-stock matrices has three irreducible dimensions: concentration across investors, concentration across stocks, and joint assignment dependence. The paper unifies previously disparate portfolio-structure measures — HHI, BSST portfolio overlap, Kolm-Ritter covariance representation, Koijen-Yogo demand system, and Kyle lambda — as special cases of the squared Frobenius norm of a derived matrix. The main results characterize how information propagates across these three dimensions.
