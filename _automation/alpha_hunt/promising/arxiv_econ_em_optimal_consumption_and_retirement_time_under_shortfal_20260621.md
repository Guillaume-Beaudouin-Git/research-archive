## Summary
An optimal portfolio, consumption, and endogenous early retirement problem is solved under a shortfall risk constraint (probability of falling below a benchmark). Using dual methods, analytical solutions show that shortfall aversion induces earlier retirement than expected utility maximization with more conservative portfolio allocation near the retirement threshold.
