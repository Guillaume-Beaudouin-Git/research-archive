## Summary
The ill-posed inverse problem of recovering a future option price profile from the current profile is studied. The forward-time Black-Scholes PDE is unstable (high-frequency amplification); regularization via Legendre polynomial truncation restores stability with provable convergence rates. The method extrapolates the current volatility surface forward without Monte Carlo.
