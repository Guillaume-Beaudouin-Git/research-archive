## Summary
The Heston, Bates, and CIR models are integrated into a single framework for pricing short-to-medium maturity equity options and assessing interest-rate risk. Calibration via Lewis Fourier inversion and Carr-Madan FFT yield nearly identical parameter sets, confirming stability. Jump intensities converge to near-zero at 60-day maturities, suggesting stochastic volatility alone explains most pricing error at that horizon.
