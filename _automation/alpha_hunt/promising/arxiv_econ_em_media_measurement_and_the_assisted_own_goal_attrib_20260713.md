## Summary
Studies "media measurement" for digital advertising — the "assisted own goal" problem where an upper-funnel platform (e.g., a short-video network) causes an incremental purchase that gets credited to a different downstream platform where the transaction is actually completed, making the true incremental value invisible to attribution-based ROAS measurement and to marketing-mix models that can only see channel-by-week aggregates. Proposes an incrementality-based alternative using per-audience randomized intent-to-treat experiments plus an individual-level extension of "Predicted Incrementality by Experimentation" (PIE) that learns a mapping from individual features to experiment-identified incremental outcomes.
