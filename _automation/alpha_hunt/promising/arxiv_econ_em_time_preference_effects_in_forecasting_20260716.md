## Summary
Shows theoretically and empirically (using forecasting-tournament data) that scoring forecasters only after an uncertain event resolves creates an incentive to report dishonestly early if forecasters discount future reward, systematically biasing forecasts of event timing (wars, recessions, eruptions) toward overstating near-term probability.
